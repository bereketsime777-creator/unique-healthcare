HOW TO USE
================================================================================

Admin - Adding PDF to New Product:
  1. Admin Panel → Products → Add New Product
  2. Fill in product details
  3. Upload product image (unchanged, optional)
  4. Scroll to "Technical Specification PDF" section
  5. Click to select PDF file
  6. Filename appears
  7. Click "Add Product"
  8. PDF stored with product

Admin - Editing Product PDF:
  1. Admin Panel → Products → Edit Product
  2. Scroll to "Technical Specification PDF" section
  3. Option A: Click to replace current PDF
  4. Option B: Click "Remove PDF" to delete
  5. Click "Save Changes"
  6. PDF updated

Customer - Downloading PDF:
  1. Go to any product page
  2. If PDF exists, see "Technical Specifications" tab
  3. Click the tab
  4. See download button with filename
  5. Click to download
  6. PDF downloads to computer
