================================================================================
HOW CLOUDINARY OPTIMIZATION WORKS
================================================================================

ORIGINAL URL:
  https://res.cloudinary.com/[cloud]/image/upload/v123/unique-healthcare-products/id

TRANSFORMATIONS APPLIED:
  f_auto    = Automatic format (WebP for modern, JPEG for old browsers)
  q_auto    = Automatic quality optimization based on content
  w_300     = Resize to 300px (cards) or 600px (details)
  dpr_auto  = Device pixel ratio scaling (1x/2x/3x)

OPTIMIZED URL:
  https://res.cloudinary.com/[cloud]/image/upload/f_auto,q_auto,w_300,dpr_auto/v123/unique-healthcare-products/id

RESULT:
  - WebP format for modern browsers (20-30% smaller)
  - JPEG fallback for older browsers
  - Optimal quality without visible compression
  - Right size for display context
  - Perfect quality on all devices

LAZY LOADING STRATEGY

ABOVE-THE-FOLD IMAGES:
  Location: ProductDetails main product image
  Loading: EAGER (no lazy attribute)

BELOW-THE-FOLD IMAGES:
  Location: Product cards in grid, featured products on home
