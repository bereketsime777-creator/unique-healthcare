## DESIGN FEATURES AVAILABLE

### Compliance Badges
Products with compliance data automatically display badges:
- EFDA (yellow)
- CE (blue)
- FDA (purple)

Add to products in admin panel: `compliance: { EFDA: true, CE: false, FDA: false }`

### Pricing Models
Two pricing options per product:
- **Fixed Price**: Shows price, "Add to Cart" button
- **Request Quote**: Shows "Request Quote" button

Set via admin: `priceType: "fixed"` or `priceType: "quote"`

### Stock Management
Products show stock status automatically:
- In Stock (green badge) - "Add to Cart" enabled
- Out of Stock (red badge) - "Add to Cart" disabled
